Privacy Policy – JackTheDripper Uploader

Last updated: 13 October 2026

JackTheDripper Uploader ("the app") is a private, non-public tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel JackTheDripperr to upload that channel's own Minecraft mob-battle videos. It is not offered to other users.

What the app uses

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth) it uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publishing time on the owner's own channel, and reads the list of the channel's own uploads to avoid duplicates.

What data is collected

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone. It does not access other users' channels, comments or viewing data. The only data it handles is the channel owner's OAuth token, which is stored locally on the owner's own computer and never sent anywhere except to Google's servers.

Google and YouTube

By using the app you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; the local token is deleted when the owner removes the app's folder.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the channel owner through the JackTheDripperr channel page or its Discord server discord.gg/Bxy5gxkq3G.
